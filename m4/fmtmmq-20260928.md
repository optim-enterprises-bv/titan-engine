# fmt-mmq: llama.cpp MMQ prefill for IQ4_NL / MXFP4 / NVFP4 in mistral.rs, 2026-09-28

The kernels are the cuda-oxide port in oxide-kernels a20ef86 (`fmt_mmq`). They are 105 entries, bit-identical to llama.cpp acecd56 (1008 comparisons, 1417 launches, mutation-tested; PORTING rules 48-49).
They are wired in mr-mmq branch `fmt-mmq` commit 2458846 (`mistralrs-quant/src/gguf/fast_mmq.rs` `llama_fmt_plain`, dispatched from `GgufMatMul::try_fast_forward` for batch > 8).
The embedded per-format PTX (`{iq4_nl,mxfp4,nvfp4}_mmq_oxide.ptx`) is byte-identical to a fresh `export_ptx.py` split.
Decode stays on candle's mmvq. `TITAN_LLAMA_MMQ=0` switches back to the dequantize + cuBLAS prefill.

Window: `flock .gpu.lock bash m4/fmtmmq-window.sh`. The log is `fmtmmq-window-20260928-0418.log` and the per-run outputs are in `fmtmmq/out/`.
titan-mistral was down from 04:18:04 to 04:39:48 (21m44s). It was serving `/v1/models` again right after.

### Prefill, ~2k-token raw prompt (2051 tokens, max_tokens=1, median of up to 5)

The table shows wall tok/s. The server's own figure is in parentheses.

| format | llama.cpp | old | deq (new build, old kernels) | **new (MMQ port)** | new / deq | new / llama.cpp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IQ4_NL | 2421 (2890) | ~337 (bigprompt 341) | 329 (333) | **~1285** (bigprompt 1285) | 3.9x | 0.53 |
| MXFP4 | 3220 (4118) | 326 (330) | 326 (330) | **1436** (1500; bigprompt 1560) | 4.4x | 0.45 |
| NVFP4 | 3724 (4977) | 324 (328) | 325 (329) | **1525** (1551; bigprompt 1602) | 4.7x | 0.41 |

The IQ4_NL old/new figures come from the server log durations of the 2k requests that succeeded (old 6.04 / 6.18 s, new 1.59 / 1.72 s) and from `bigprompt.py`. The first version of `prefill2k.py` aborted on the first HTTP 500 (see below) and was made tolerant during the window.

Decode is unchanged, as expected: new 57.5 / 56.4 / 53.2 tok/s, old 51.6 / 50.3 / 47.7, llama.cpp 66.5 / 64.6 / 65.8 (8x64 greedy).

### Greedy text (8 prompts x 64 tokens, leading whitespace ignored)

| format | llama vs old | llama vs **new** | old vs new | new vs deq |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IQ4_NL | 7/8 | 6/8 | 7/8 | 7/8 |
| MXFP4 | 3/8 | 2/8 | 4/8 | 4/8 |
| NVFP4 | 4/8 | **6/8** | 5/8 | 5/8 |

- **NVFP4** moves toward llama.cpp: 4 -> 6/8. Its remaining two divergences (chars 69 and 42) are shared with the old path.
- **IQ4_NL** keeps its shared divergence (prompt 2, char 149). It adds one late divergence (prompt 1, char 110 of 232).
- **MXFP4** already diverged early on the old path, on 4 of the same prompts at the same characters (0, 15, 118, 168). It changes prompt 5 from an early divergence (char 7) to a late one (char 111), and prompt 1 now diverges at char 5 (`<= 0` vs `== 0`).

On MXFP4 the other layers dominate (attention, norms, the Q8_0/Q6_K tensors, FP4 activation rounding at near-ties). This was already true in `mxfp4-real-20260928.md` (5/8 on the real MoE model).

The kernels themselves are bit-identical in isolation (the gate). Whole-model greedy agreement is not a gate for this change.

## Known issue seen in the window (not caused by this change)

Both mistral.rs builds, old and new, and with the MMQ port on or off, intermittently fail a 2k-token prompt with `CUDA_ERROR_OUT_OF_MEMORY`. This happens with the dense 14B at `--max-seq-len 4096` on the 16 GB card, typically on the second or third back-to-back 2k request; the next request succeeds. llama.cpp had none.

The deq and old runs failed 2 of 7 requests, and new failed 2 to 3 of 8. The fix belongs to the KV / reserve sizing work, not to fmt-mmq.
