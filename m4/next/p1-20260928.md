W1 profile 86 us. The 80B CPU side is page-fault / NVMe bound, more so the less RAM is free.

## 35B mmap cost (40 x 256 vs m3/out/q35-prof.json, all 40/40)

| run | tok/s | minflt in CPU passes | storage read |
|---|---|---|---|
| owned (W1 / W2) | 80.0 / 78.0 | 2.6k | 22 GB |
| mmap | 67.9 | 462k | 29 GB |
| mmap, no WILLNEED | 67.0 | 343k (+85k major) | 34 GB |
| mmap, MADV_HUGEPAGE | 67.2 | 482k | 30 GB |
| mmap, populate at load | 73.5 | 103k | 19 GB |
| mmap, pageout (W2) | 75.0 | 308k | 26 GB |
| mmap, pageout + populate (W2) | 77.1 | 15k | 8 GB |
| mmap, pageout + populate + P1 L+2 (W2) | 71.2 | 10k | 22 GB |

Cause: the GPU-resident experts are read through the mmap at load and stay in the page cache charged to the
server's cgroup, so host experts get reclaimed and re-faulted; plus first-touch faults. Not the WILLNEED calls
(dropping them is slower) and not the TLB (file THP does not happen). P1 costs the 35B (93% hit, all in RAM).
