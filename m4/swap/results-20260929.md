# m4/swap: in-process model swapping for titan-mistral, 2026-09-29

Branch `model-swap` off `titan-094` (f601844a6) in `~/titan-engine/mr-swap`. The commits are
c798d15f2, 11ab4c51f and 90e86697e. The build is nvcc-free (`--features oxide`) into
`target-swap-oxide`.

## What changed

- **Per-model settings.** Every `TITAN_*` read goes through `mistralrs_quant::titan_cfg::var`:
  - the loading model's `[models.titan.env]` table is checked first, then the process env
    (an empty value unsets a name);
  - cached settings moved from `OnceLock` to `titan_cfg::GenCell`, which recomputes once per
    settings generation (each `begin_model` / `end_model`);
  - there are about 60 cached settings across tiered, doorbell, onepass, pfs, flash
    decode/prefill, attention, qwen35 prefill/MTP, graphs, the prefix cache and the auto plan.
- **Swap mode** (`mistralrs-core/src/titan_swap.rs`), turned on by `[titan_swap]` in a
  `from-config` TOML:
  - Every `[[models]]` entry is registered, and only the default loads at start.
  - A request for an unloaded model takes the swap lock and evicts the LRU resident model
    (`max_resident = 1`). The eviction waits for in-flight requests, joins the engine thread
    and drops the pipeline. It then stops the titan threads and frees their buffers, and trims
    the CUDA pools and mimalloc.
  - After the eviction, the swap installs the model's settings and loads it.
  - Requests that arrive during a swap wait on the lock.
  - Absent, unknown and `default` names go to the default model.
  - `idle_ttl_secs` is an optional per-model auto-unload.
  - `/v1/models` and the UI list every model with its status. `/v1/models/{unload,reload}` go
    through the swap.
- **Released on unload:**
  - the doorbell (host thread joined, mapped memory and device counter freed);
  - the miss spin pool (workers stopped) and the lookahead prefetch workers;
  - the pfs staging ring and copy worker (pinned bounce buffers freed, host stores unregistered
    in their context);
  - the fast_mmvq / fast_mmq / gguf-cuda workspaces, including the outgrown ones graphs used to
    pin;
  - the top-1 workspace, and the counters;
  - `AUTO_FRACTION` / `AUTO_EXTRA_RESERVE`.

  The graph pools, the GDN state pool, the prefix-cache entries and the mmaps go with the
  model and engine.
- **Auto plan.** Every load plans from the first load's free VRAM. After the first model,
  about 156 MiB stays in the context for good (kernel modules, library workspaces). A fresh
  process allocates the same during its first load. Without this, the 35B planned 0.52 instead
  of 0.54 after a swap, and identity dropped to 5/8.
- **CUDA OOM recovery** (live incident). A step that fails with OUT_OF_MEMORY fails only its
  requests. Their caches and the prefix cache are dropped, and the model's graphs, the staging
  ring and the doorbell are reset. The device is synced and the pools trimmed.
- **Fixed on the way:**
  - The TOML device options defaulted to `max_batch_size = 0`, which planned the KV reserve as
    0. opencode then OOMed.
  - The doorbell's 4-byte counter pinned one 32 MiB pool chunk per load.

## Live "regression" after the deploy, and the redeploy (window 5, 16:45-16:50)

- **Report.** After the deploy, live opencode requests failed with OOM and the coordinator rolled back to pf3.
- **Root cause.** It was not the swap build. The failing request was a **64,720-token prompt**
  (`resume at 22896 of 64720 prompt tokens`, `content_length=183422`).
  - The 16-23k requests before it succeeded. At 16k and 22.7k they fell back to CPU-twin
    prefill, but they completed.
  - opencode sent 64.7k because the titan context limit had just been raised from 36864 to
    65536, so it stopped compacting.
  - The KV cache at 64k plus the pfs headroom (1920 MiB = 768 + 512 for tasks + 640 for 64k
    context) does not fit.
  - opencode then resent the same 64.7k prompt on every retry.
- **Evidence.** The same cold-prompt sequence, run in one window on the test port:

  | build | 13k | 23k | 28k | 36k | 64k | 13k after 64k |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | pf3 (rollback binary + env) | 2623 | 2476 | 2357 | 2036 | OOM | **314** (no recovery) |
  | swap, fresh | 2458 | 2463 | 2419 | 2084 | - | - |
  | swap, after a swap to bonsai and back | 2464 | 2326 | 2322 | 2096 | OOM | **2592** |

  - Prompt tok/s. Neither build logged "no room" through 36k.
  - The auto plan was the same on both: 15565 MiB free, fraction 0.54.
  - The pfs ring lines of pf3 and the fresh swap server match line for line: 4/3/2/1 x 96 MiB
    with 2196/1904/1873/1857 MiB free.
  - 64k OOMs on both builds.
  - pf3 is left degraded after the OOM; the swap build recovers.
- **Action.**
  - opencode's titan context limit is back to 36864. The 65536 version is saved as
    `opencode.json.bak-65k-20260929`.
  - The swap unit was redeployed (22 s restart).
  - Live check: opencode `ok`, `/v1/models` lists 6 models, `/ui` returns 200.
- **Still needed for 64k.** The pfs headroom rework from m4/pf4 (efc30872a, WIP).
